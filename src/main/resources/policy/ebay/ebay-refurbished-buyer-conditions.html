What does “refurbished” mean?

A refurbished product is one that has been expertly restored and tested by the manufacturer or qualified seller. Products are cleaned, repaired, and reconditioned to 100% functionality and then repackaged with new accessories.

How is Refurbished different from Used?

eBay Refurbished items are from quality-vetted sellers who adhere to our strict grading and service criteria. All Refurbished items are put through a multi-point inspection, and each purchase comes with a warranty. Used items may have some signs of cosmetic wear, but they are fully operational and function as intended. Review the full Item Condition definitions.

What is the difference between manufacturer refurbished, authorized-seller refurbished, and seller refurbished?

eBay Refurbished products are graded under Certified, Excellent, Very Good or Good condition tiers. Certified condition items can either come from the manufacturer/brand who originally made the item, or an authorized reseller who has a license or agreement with the manufacturer to sell their products. Excellent, Very Good, and Good condition items come from third-party sellers that eBay has vetted, monitors, and holds to strict standards. The “Seller Refurbished” item condition is not part of the official eBay Refurbished program, rather is a listing condition as defined by an individual seller. See seller's listing for full details.

How does the eBay Refurbished warranty work?

Every eBay Refurbished purchase comes with a warranty, serviced by Allstate. Certified condition items come with a two-year warranty; Excellent, Very Good and Good condition items come with a one-year warranty. Warranty coverage will begin on the date of purchase. Allstate will service all warranty claims directly.

Your warranty is automatically registered with Allstate at the time of purchase and is included at no extra cost with the purchase of an eBay Refurbished item. You will receive a separate email with details of your warranty. If for any reason you do not receive the warranty confirmation email, the eBay Refurbished badge can act as proof of warranty when paired with a receipt. 

For more information related to the warranty, including coverage details and how to file a claim, visit our eBay Refurbished Warranty Page.

What if there is a defect with my order?

We’ve got you covered! All eBay Refurbished items are expertly refurbished by professionals who meet our strict quality standards. However, in the rare event that your order arrives with a defect, with limited exceptions, you have 30 days to return the item for free. eBay Refurbished items are also covered by a one- or two-year warranty

What if my order doesn’t match the condition grade?

You can compare the condition grade you ordered with our eBay Refurbished grading criteria. If they do not match, with limited exceptions, you can return the item for free within 30 days of purchase. Learn about Returns.

*What if I’m not happy with my eBay Refurbished purchase?

You have 30 days to return an eBay Refurbished item for free, subject to certain exceptions. To learn more about eBay’s hassle-free returns, click here. eBay Refurbished items are also backed by eBay’s Money Back Guarantee.
