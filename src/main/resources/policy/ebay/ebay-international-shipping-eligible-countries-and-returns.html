Introducing eBay International Shipping

Delivering better benefits to simplify international shipping

Save when you ship from sellers in the US, Canada and the UK.

eBay International Shipping is the easy way for you to shop from sellers in the U.S.,Canada and the UK, no matter where you live. Thanks to eBay International Shipping, you will generally see lower shipping costs* and have peace of mind with eBay handling any returns and managing refunds.

Key Benefits

  • Shipping you can trust

    With eBay managing the international shipping process, including customs and duties, your item will be shipped with trustworthy carriers, and delivered on time.

  • Pay less for shipping

    You will generally see lower shipping costs with eBay International Shipping*.

  • Access to millions of unique items

    Thanks to eBay International Shipping you can access a huge variety of items. Millions of new listings are now at your fingertips, wherever you are in the world.

  • No return hassles

    If you need to open a return, eBay will manage the process on behalf of the seller. 30-day returns are offered on all items shipped through eBay International Shipping.

  • Tracking for every order

    All eBay International Shipping orders include tracking, enabling you to monitor the progress of your package throughout its journey.

  • *Compared to the non-negotiated cost of shipping with many other international carriers; eBay has negotiated competitive shipping costs for eBay International Shipping. Total international shipping cost is subject to change and depends on the destination country, weight, and dimensions of an item.

Shop the millions of items now available to ship from sellers in the U.S., Canada and the UK to over 200 countries.

eBay will manage delivery to your door, including customs paperwork.

And if something goes wrong, eBay will manage any issues, including returns.

FAQs

eBay International Shipping is a new shipping service that removes the hassle associated with international shipping. eBay handles tracking, customs clearance, and returns. You will generally see lower shipping costs* and be able to track the status of your order from your eBay account.

*Compared to the non-negotiated cost of shipping with many other international carriers; eBay has negotiated competitive shipping costs for eBay International Shipping. Total international shipping cost is subject to change and depends on the destination country, weight, and dimensions of an item.

Items purchased from abroad might be subject to import duties and taxes that a buyer has to pay. Application of duties and taxes and the amount depends on the destination country, item price and item weight.

At the moment, eBay International Shipping is available on most items up to $2,500 item value (excluding taxes, duties, and delivery fees) and located in the U.S., Canada and the UK. However, items included in eBay’s hazardous, restricted or regulated materials policy, and items that don’t follow our International trading policy are not available with eBay International Shipping. View a list of restricted or prohibited categories.

Note: This is not an exhaustive list and it may be updated based on transportation regulations and export laws, and on macro environment or international regulations.
