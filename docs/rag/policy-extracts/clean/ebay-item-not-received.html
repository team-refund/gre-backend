Get help with an item that hasn't arrived

Paraphrased evidence extract from eBay US Help. Source checked 2026-11-28.

Missing delivery or part of an order

After the estimated delivery date passes, a buyer can report a missing item from Purchases. The buyer can ask the seller for tracking information, a replacement, or a refund to the original payment method. eBay advises reporting within 30 calendar days of the delivery date to retain eligibility. Report a separately purchased missing item as not received. If a component of a set or bundle is missing, open a return request for the set.

Tracking and follow-up

For tracking marked delivered, the buyer can check the checkout address and carrier delivery photo, ask the carrier for details, and tell the seller what was checked. eBay assistance is available after three business days without resolution, up to 21 business days from opening the original request.
