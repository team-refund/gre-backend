The parts and accessories that fit, or your money back

With eBay Guaranteed Fit, when you purchase an eligible automotive part or accessory, we guarantee it will fit your vehicle or you can return it for free.

How it works

Search by vehicle

Use the Find Parts & Accessories tool on the eBay Motors homepage or select your saved vehicle in My Garage to search for parts that fit.

We’ll show you parts that fit

We will verify the provided vehicle details match the item compatibility information provided by our seller. When there is a match, fit is confirmed for that specific item and is indicated by a checkmark on the listing.

Purchase an eligible part or accessory

When you make a purchase of an eligible part or accessory with the checkmark, fit is guaranteed. Exclusions may apply.

We’ll protect you

If the part or accessory you purchased does not fit your vehicle, you can return it for free within 30 days of receiving it for a full refund.

What items are excluded from eBay Guaranteed Fit?

Tires and wheels are currently excluded from the eBay Guaranteed Fit program. We will still confirm which tires and wheels are compatible for your vehicle using the checkmark. Your tires and wheels may still be eligible for a return under our eBay Money Back Guarantee. Learn more.

How to search for the right part?

Use the Find Parts & Accessories tool
If you start from eBay Motors’ homepage, you can input your vehicle details in the Find Parts & Accessories tool. Just enter the year/make/model/trim/engine and click Find Parts.


Add a vehicle or select a vehicle in My Garage
If you start from eBay Motors’ homepage, you can also add a vehicle into My Garage by inputting your vehicle’s year/make/model/trim/engine or select an existing vehicle saved in My Garage and search for the part you are looking for. If you have multiple vehicles saved in My Garage, please ensure you have selected the vehicle for which you are shopping parts and accessories.


Add a vehicle or select a vehicle on the search page
If you start by searching a part or accessory in the search bar, you need to select an existing vehicle you have saved in My Garage or enter another vehicle for us to check the parts that fit. If you have multiple vehicles saved in My Garage, be sure to choose the vehicle you are shopping for.

How do returns work for eBay Guaranteed Fit?

You can start a return from your Purchases in My eBay. When selecting your return reason, choose “Doesn’t fit my vehicle” for a free return shipping label. If you don’t receive your label after the return is accepted, ask us to step in and help.

Why can’t I find the return reason “Doesn’t fit my vehicle”?

You will only see the reason “Doesn’t fit my vehicle” when the item you purchased is eligible for eBay Guaranteed Fit protection.

How long do I have to open a return?

In order to be eligible for protection, you must open the return request within 30 calendar days after the delivery date or within the seller’s stated return window, whichever is longer. Learn more about eBay Money Back Guarantee and return requirements.

After I ship my return, when can I expect my refund?

Most sellers submit a refund as soon as they receive the returned item. If the item has been returned to the seller and they have not given you a full refund after 2 business days, you can ask us to step in and help.

What if I have an issue that’s not fitment related, what protections do I have?

eBay Money Back Guarantee covers most transactions on eBay. It means you can get your money back if an item didn’t arrive, is faulty or damaged, or doesn’t match the listing. Learn more about eBay Money Back Guarantee.
