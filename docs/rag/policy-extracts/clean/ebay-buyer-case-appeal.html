Appeal the outcome of a case as a buyer

Paraphrased evidence extract from eBay US Help. Source checked 2026-11-28.

Deadline and new evidence

A buyer can appeal an eBay decision about a return or missing item within 30 calendar days of the case closing by submitting new information. Relevant evidence can include photographs showing how the item differs from the listing, return tracking showing receipt by the seller, or carrier evidence showing delivery to the wrong address.

Submitting the appeal

Open the relevant purchase, go to the case details, and use the appeal option to submit the new evidence. The appeal is sent to eBay for review. A buyer preparing a message to the seller can use the evidence examples to identify which records to request or attach.
